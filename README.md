# Hi, I'm Philipp 👋

I build software and infrastructure that connects the physical and the digital world —
from PLCs, machines and embedded hardware to the backends, platforms and dashboards that make their data useful.

Computer Science student at the University of Innsbruck · Automation & Digitalization at INNIO · Tyrol, Austria

---

## What I'm Working On

- **Industrial Data:** Connecting PLCs, CNC machines and robots via OPC UA, Modbus and MQTT to automated data pipelines
- **Platform & Infrastructure:** Self-hosted Kubernetes with GitOps (ArgoCD), segmented networks, reverse proxies and Cloudflare Tunnels
- **Full-Stack Projects:** FastAPI backends and React frontends — containerized, tested and deployed end-to-end
- **Always building something:** IoT projects with Raspberry Pi and ESP32, self-hosted on my own infrastructure
